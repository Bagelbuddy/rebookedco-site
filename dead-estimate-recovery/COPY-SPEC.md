## 10. FAQ

**Q: Will every dead quote come back?**
A: No, and anyone who promises that is lying to you. The honest range is 5% to 12%, and we plan your numbers at 10%. On a typical book that is still five figures a year. And since you only pay per job that books and pays, the ones who never answer cost you zero.

**Q: Is this spammy?**
A: These are people who called you and asked for a price. The follow-up goes out under your name, you approve every word before it sends, and anyone who says stop is never contacted again. There is no automated platform blasting your customer list. It is your voice, our elbow grease.

**Q: What do I actually have to do?**
A: Three things. Send one export of your unsold estimates, approve the messages we write, and run the jobs that book. Nothing to learn, nothing to install, nobody new on payroll.

**Q: What does it cost?**
A: The audit is free, full stop. If you want us to recover the money, a $500 deposit holds your spot and credits 100% against fees. Then you pay per recovered job: the greater of $250 or 20% of that job's first-year value, only after it books, gets serviced, and pays. The ongoing Follow-Up Desk is $400 a month if you want it.

**Q: Why is the audit free?**
A: Because it is how we earn the job. The audit shows you the exact dollars in your drawer, with the math visible. If the number is small, we tell you and part friends. If it is big, you will want us to go get it. We make money when you do, not before.

**Q: How is this different from your Dead List Profit Audit?**
A: That one revives lapsed customers you have not serviced in a while. This one chases estimates that never closed. Different list, same rule: we bring back money you already paid to create.

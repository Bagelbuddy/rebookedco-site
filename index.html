How it works

One layer between the front door and the team.

Rebooked sits on top of the tools you already use. It does not replace your practice management system or field service software; it keeps conversations and work from falling between them.

01

AI front desk

Answers inbound calls and texts, collects the details your team needs, and books into open windows you define.

02

Internal handoffs

When a request needs a person, it lands in a shared inbox with context attached: who called, what they need, what was already said.

03

Scheduling

Chairs, crews or technicians: the same view shows what is booked, what is pending and what still needs a confirm.

04

Follow-up

Recall reminders, estimate follow-ups and seasonal service nudges, sent on rules your team approves.

05

Audits

A reviewable log of every automated message and handoff, so you can check what was said and when.

Privacy-first

Your records stay yours.

The data belongs to the practice or the company. Rebooked is designed to work with it, not to collect it.

Company-owned

Customer and patient records live in your systems and your workspace.

Minimal by design

The AI layer only needs what it takes to answer, route and schedule.

Reviewable

Audit logs let owners see exactly what the AI did on their behalf.
